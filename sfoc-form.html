SORA 2.5 — supporting risk assessment (Appendix A)
What is a SAIL level?
The Specific Assurance and Integrity Level (SAIL) is the output of the SORA risk assessment. It ranges from I (lowest risk) to VI (highest risk) and determines the robustness of evidence Transport Canada will require for your SFOC application. It is calculated — not chosen — from your Ground Risk Class and Air Risk Class below.
